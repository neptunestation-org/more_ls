# Lean track (D11, D41)

`lean/` is a Lake project (core Lean 4, no Mathlib; pinned in
`lean/lean-toolchain`). `cd lean && lake build` checks it; CI does, and `uv run pytest -m lean`
does when `lake` is on PATH (skipped otherwise). `lean/MLS/Pred.lean` is `logic/tvl.py`
transcribed clause for clause and proved sound against the SQL meaning; `lean/MLS/Policy.lean`
transcribes `props/effective.py`, the statement outcomes of `pg/model.py` and the assumption
builders of `props/obligations.py`; `lean/MLS/Normalize.lean` is the NULL normalization of the
CHECK and transition templates (and of their `*_NAIVE` mutations); `lean/MLS/Relational.lean`
is the CHECK and FK templates against a relational model, with the RESTRICT guard and
relation-change axioms of `obligations.py` derived from it; `lean/MLS/Transition.lean` is the
transition trigger against that model (the induction step of `invariant_preserved` lifted to
the state), `lean/MLS/Reach.lean` the compiled hierarchy helper and depth trigger,
`lean/MLS/Cardinality.lean` the cardinality trigger over a bag of rows. When you change any of
them, change the transcription and re-prove, or record the gap under "Known gaps" below.
`lean/MLS/Vectors.lean` is generated by `uv run python -m tests.lean.vectors` (D43): `#guard`s
comparing `Pred.eval`, `Pred.tf` and the statement outcomes with the Python evaluator, encoder
and oracle on the same data; the fast suite checks it is current, so regenerate it when you
change `pg/model.py`, `logic/tvl.py` or the generator. No local toolchain is needed: a throwaway container
works (CI uses `leanprover/lean-action`).

## Known gaps

What a Python change left untranscribed.  Remove an entry when the transcription catches up.

- **The relation row axiom (#24).**  `props/obligations.py:relation_row_axioms` (a stored row of a
  base relation's table that an obligation reads is in the relation, SEMANTICS §7) is not
  transcribed.  `Relational.lean` derives a relation from the stored rows, where it holds by
  construction; the `Pred`-level environment the encoder's soundness is stated over takes the
  relations as given, so `Pred.tf_sound` neither uses nor states it.  Adding it is an extra
  hypothesis tying `Env.rel` to the rows the obligation reads, as `chainPy_eq` does for hops.
- **Relation conditions (#32).**  A base relation's `where` (D61) is not transcribed: `Relational.lean`
  derives a relation from all the stored rows, and the row axiom with a condition
  (`rel(row.key, row.value) ⇔ T(cond(row))`, SEMANTICS §7) is the untranscribed row axiom above
  with the condition's three-valued truth on the right.  The compiled subquery is the same
  `= any(array(...))` with the condition conjoined, so the gap is the relation's definition, not
  a new evaluation rule.  The subset axiom (#37: a filtered relation's facts are among its
  unfiltered twin's, `obligations.subset_axioms`) follows from that definition and is not
  transcribed either.
- **Deferred inclusions (#35).**  A deferred inclusion's foreign key (D75) is checked at commit.
  `Relational.lean`'s inclusion theorems (`inclusion_of` and the per-statement ones) are about the
  immediate template, where every statement keeps the inclusion; a deferred one holds only on
  committed states, and no transcription states a transaction.  The proofs leave it out of the
  stored-row constraints and the relation axioms, which only weakens their assumptions.
- **The enum domain axiom (#30).**  `props/obligations.py:enum_row_axioms` (an enum column of a
  row an obligation reads is NULL or one of its type's labels, SEMANTICS §7) is not transcribed.
  Columns are `Int`-valued with interned text constants (`Term.const`), so the axiom is an extra
  hypothesis on the valuation, like the relation row axiom: `Pred.tf_sound` neither uses nor
  states it, and the encoder (`logic/tvl.py`) is unchanged.  The same holds for an enum context
  value (#89): the axiom that it is unset or one of its labels is a hypothesis on `Env`, not
  transcribed.

- **The bool domain axiom (#99).**  `props/obligations.py:bool_row_axioms` (a bool column of a row
  an obligation reads, and a bool context value, is NULL or 0 or 1) is not transcribed, for the
  enum axiom's reason: columns are `Int`-valued, so it is an extra hypothesis on the valuation that
  `Pred.tf_sound` neither uses nor states.  It only strengthens the obligations' assumptions to the
  states Postgres can hold.

What each step states rather than proves (sets rather than bags outside `Cardinality.lean`,
self-referencing FKs, the `SECURITY DEFINER` helper seeing every row, `FOR UPDATE` serializing
writers) is in `docs/design/archive/ROADMAP.md`, "Parallel track — Lean 4".
- **Hops up a foreign key (#43) and through membership tables (#33, D76).**  `Pred.memberVia` is
  the exact chain; the encoder's hop with no lookup back
  (an uninterpreted `up.<chain>` with the one-way row axioms `obligations.up_row_axioms`, D69) is
  not transcribed, nor is `chain_fact`'s split between lookups and `up` symbols.  Its soundness
  argument is the base relation's: the proofs hold for every relation the axioms allow, and the
  real chain satisfies them.
- **Every writer's update (#40, D77).**  `obligations.always_on_update` (the constraints on both
  images and the transition rules imply a two-state predicate, no policies) is not transcribed in
  `Policy.lean`.  It is the update case of `Transition.lean`'s step with a goal over both images
  instead of an invariant of the new one.
- **Column guards (#50, D78).**  `obligations.guard_terms` (an update by the guard's role of a row
  its condition is TRUE of keeps every other column) and `Oracle.guards_ok` are not transcribed:
  `Policy.lean`'s update assumptions have the column privileges but no guard, and its statement
  outcomes no guard check.  The guard is the column-privilege predicate under an antecedent.
  The `changes_only` property (#56, D80) is the same update assumptions implying the unchanged
  columns, so it waits on the guard term.
- **Cascade inclusions (#60, D79).**  `obligations.cascade_obligations` (a cascaded delete is one
  the role's delete policies on the referring table allow) and the oracle's skipped RESTRICT
  check on a cascade delete are not transcribed: `Relational.lean`'s FK guard is RESTRICT only.
  Nor is set_null (#62, D81): the induction step's nulled-row case, `_set_null_obligations` and the
  oracle's `_set_null_ok`.
- **Relations without a key (#63, D84)** are not transcribed: `Pred.member` takes a key.  A set is
  the relation with every fact's key a fixed constant, so the transcription is `Pred.member` with a
  constant key and the set's row axiom, the untranscribed relation row axiom above.
- **Inclusions into a relation with a condition (#54, D86)** are not transcribed: `Relational.lean`'s
  inclusion is a foreign key onto every row of the target.  The triggers' at-commit check is the
  deferred inclusion's gap above with the relation's condition gap: the predicate is the same.
- **Cardinality `where` and `min` (#61, D83)** are not transcribed: `Cardinality.lean` counts every
  row of a group against a maximum; the condition on the counted rows and the commit-time minimum
  (which the proofs do not use) are not modelled.
- **Lookup helpers (#34) and nullable membership values (#42)** need no transcription: the helper
  is a compilation of the same atom, and `liftCmp` already makes a NULL value UNKNOWN.
- **Relations over nullable columns (#49, D71).**  `Relational.lean` derives a relation from every
  stored row; the encoder's NULL filter (`¬null(key) ∧ ¬null(value)` in the row axiom) and the
  visibility obligation's `¬null(key)` are not transcribed.  Lookups keyed by a constant (D70)
  need nothing: a helper is a compilation of the same atom.
- **Nested filters and a unique value's one row (#51, D72).**  The subset axiom between two
  filtered relations whose conditions imply each other, and `∀k: rel(k, x.value) → k = x.key` for a
  unique value, are not transcribed.  Both follow from `Relational.lean`'s derivation of a relation
  from the stored rows (plus the key constraint), which is where they would be proved.
- **Array columns in CHECK and transition theorems (#65, D88).**  `Pred.has` is transcribed in
  `Pred.lean` (eval, encoder, `tf_sound`; vectors in `Vectors.lean`), but `Pred.rowOnly` and
  `Pred.twoImage` leave it out, so `Relational.lean` and `Transition.lean` do not cover a CHECK or
  transition that tests an array.  Including it needs `Env.arr` equal across the two environments
  those theorems compare (`Env.ofRow` / `Env.ofImages` would carry it from `Extra`).
- **Column defaults in inserts (#76, D96).**  `obligations.insert_written` (an insert by a role that
  may not name a column writes that column's declared default) and the oracle's filled row are not
  transcribed: `Policy.lean`'s insert outcome takes the whole row as written.  The default is an
  extra equation on the new image, as the column privileges are for an update.
